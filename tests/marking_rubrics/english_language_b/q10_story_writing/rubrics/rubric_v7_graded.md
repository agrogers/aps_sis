# Q10 Generic Graded Checklist (v7_graded)

Total 30 = AO4 (10 items, each 0-2, max 20) + AO5 (5 items, each 0-2, max 10).
Strands follow the official AO4 (purpose, intended reader, form, tone, register) and AO5 (structure, vocabulary, sentence variety, paragraphing, accuracy).

## Scale for every item
- 2 = SECURE: present, working, and sustained across the piece. Quote two separate instances.
- 1 = PARTIAL: present once, or present but its effect is unclear or undermined in one place. Quote one instance.
- 0 = ABSENT, or undermined by a lapse a reader would notice. Quote the lapse if there is one.

## Rules
- Score on the words of the answer only. Do not reward length, effort, or ambition.
- An attempt at a device is not enough: it must produce an effect a reader would feel.
- A central thread the narrator raises and the story never answers (or a major event with no cause) lowers A4 to 1 or 0 unless the gap is clearly deliberate.
- Form-neutral: the items apply to a story, diary, letter, speech, or monologue alike.

## AO4 (10 items)

Purpose and reader
A1 HOOK: do the opening lines create a question, tension or image the reader wants answered? 0 = opening summarises background ("It all began when...").
A2 RESPONSE: are at least two passages aimed at a named reader response (e.g. suspense, amusement, unease, sympathy, surprise) and do they achieve it?
A3 REGISTER: is the language suited to the intended reader throughout, with no shift that a reader would notice?

Form and structure
A4 SEQUENCE: does each major event follow from what came before, so the reader never has to guess a link? Count unexplained links.
A5 SHIFT: does something change in what the reader understands (reveal, reversal, reinterpretation of an earlier moment)? It must be prepared earlier to score 2.
A6 SCENE: are key moments dramatised with specific action, detail and speech, rather than reported in summary? 0 = mostly summary.
A7 PACE: is length varied on purpose in more than one place, with a clear effect each time?

Tone and effect
A8 VOICE: does one controlled voice or tone do work through the whole piece (humour, irony, menace, tenderness, etc.)? 0 = the voice is flat or moralising.
A9 DEVICES: are three or more different language devices (imagery, figurative language, repetition, contrast, irony, direct address, speech) used for a stated effect?
A10 ENDING: do the final lines produce an effect the piece has earned (an image, an echo, a reversal, a resolved question)? 0 = the ending states a lesson, summarises, or simply stops.

## AO5 (5 items)
B1 VOCABULARY: are word choices precise and varied, doing specific work rather than generic or repeated?
B2 SENTENCES: is there a range of sentence types and lengths, controlled so that each choice serves the meaning?
B3 PARAGRAPHS: does each paragraph organise one moment or idea, with breaks that match the content and create effect? 0 = one block, or breaks that confuse.
B4 ACCURACY: judge the effect of errors on the reader, not their count. 2 = errors are rare or minor and never stop the reader; 1 = errors are noticeable across the piece but the meaning is always clear; 0 = errors make a passage hard to follow or change the meaning.
B5 FLUENCY: do sentences read smoothly, without tangled or unclear sentences the reader must reread?

## Output (one line per answer, E1 to E6)
ID | A1..A10 (0-2) | AO4 (sum, 0-20) | B1..B5 (0-2) | AO5 (sum, 0-10) | TOTAL (0-30) | one quote for the strongest 2 and one for the weakest 0 or 1
