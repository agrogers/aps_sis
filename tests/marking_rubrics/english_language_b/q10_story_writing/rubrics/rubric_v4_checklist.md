# Q10 Creative Writing Checklist Rubric (v4_checklist)

Total 30 = AO4 (10 items x 2 marks = 20) + AO5 (5 items x 2 marks = 10).

## Rules for every item
- An item is MET only if you quote the exact sentence or phrase that shows it. No quote means NOT MET.
- Do not infer intent. Mark what is on the page.
- Mark each item independently. Do not adjust one item because another is met.
- Count, then total. Do not add or subtract marks for any other reason.

## AO4 checklist (communication and structure, 10 items, 2 marks each)
A1 HOOK: the first two sentences create a question, tension, a strong image, or direct speech.
A2 WITHHELD: at least one piece of information is held back so the reader is curious or misled, and it is revealed later.
A3 REVEAL: a later moment recasts an earlier event, character, or assumption (a twist, a reversal, or a turn that changes what the reader thought).
A4 CALLBACK: a line, image, phrase, or idea from the opening or an earlier point returns later with a changed or sharper meaning.
A5 ENDING LANDS: the last sentence or paragraph is an effect (a punchline, image, or reversal), not a summary or a moral statement ("the beginning of a happy life", "I am Hamza", "If you are overstimulating").
A6 PACE: at least two deliberate short sentences or one-line paragraphs used for effect (e.g. "Regret.", "Could it be?").
A7 DIALOGUE: at least two lines of direct speech that move the plot or show character.
A8 SUSTAINED VOICE: a consistent tone (humour, irony, suspense or pathos) carried through at least four paragraphs.
A9 SCENE NOT SUMMARY: at least two moments are dramatised with specific sensory or physical detail, not reported in one line ("It all began when..." or "they were reliefed" count as summary).
A10 NO UNEXPLAINED LEAPS: the plot moves in steps the reader can follow. Mark NOT MET if a major event happens with no cause, or time jumps without explanation (e.g. a result or outcome that appears with no preceding action).

## AO5 checklist (vocabulary and accuracy, 5 items, 2 marks each)
B1 VOCABULARY: at least three precise or ambitious word choices used for effect (quote them).
B2 SENTENCE VARIETY: at least three different sentence types appear (short, compound, complex, question, exclamation), each used for a reason.
B3 PARAGRAPHING: at least four paragraphs, each focused on one moment or idea.
B4 TENSE CONSISTENCY: the tense is held throughout. Mark NOT MET for any unintended tense shift (quote one).
B5 CLARITY: no spelling, punctuation or grammar error stops the reader following the text. Minor slips do not fail this item. Quote any error that does.

## Scoring
AO4 = (number of A items MET) x 2. AO5 = (number of B items MET) x 2. TOTAL = AO4 + AO5.

## Output format (one line per answer)
ID | A1..A10 (MET/NOT) | AO4 | B1..B5 (MET/NOT) | AO5 | TOTAL | one quote for the most important MET item and one for the most important NOT MET item
