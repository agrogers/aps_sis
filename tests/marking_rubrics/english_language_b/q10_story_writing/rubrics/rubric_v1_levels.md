# Q10 Creative Writing Marking Rubric (v1_levels)

Total 30 marks = AO4 (20: communication, structure, purpose, audience, tone, effect) + AO5 (10: vocabulary, sentence control, paragraphing, accuracy).

## Process (follow in order, for each answer)
1. Read the whole answer once before scoring. Do not reward length in itself.
2. Decide the AO4 LEVEL (1-5) first, using the descriptors below. Choose the level that best describes the answer overall. A level is held only if most of its features are secure; a single strong moment does not lift a level.
3. Within the level, choose the MARK from the band (AO4 bands: L1 1-4, L2 5-8, L3 9-12, L4 13-16, L5 17-20). Use the lower half of the band if the level's features are only partly secure, the upper half if they are firm and consistent.
4. Repeat steps 2-3 for AO5 (L1 1-2, L2 3-4, L3 5-6, L4 7-8, L5 9-10).
5. Total = AO4 + AO5. Quote one short phrase for each strength and weakness that drove your decision.

## AO4 descriptors (communication and structure, 20 marks)
Ask four questions: (a) Is the narrative controlled from opening to ending? (b) Are events developed, with detail and cause and effect, rather than summarised? (c) Are devices (pace, short sentences, shifts of focus, hooks, withheld information, reversal, humour, a deliberate ending) used for a purpose? (d) Is the tone consistent and suited to the reader?

- L1 (1-4): Little narrative shape. Events are listed or summarised in order. Little or no reader awareness. Tone unclear or inconsistent. Ending absent or arbitrary.
- L2 (5-8): Some structure, with a basic opening and ending. Events are developed unevenly, with some detail. Attempts at effect are generic ("it was scary"). Tone is mostly consistent but not controlled. Some sections feel disconnected.
- L3 (9-12): Clear, mostly controlled structure. Events developed with relevant detail. Some deliberate effects (pace, imagery, dialogue) that work. Tone suits the reader. Weaknesses include a rushed or unresolved section, a loose ending, or unexplained leaps in the plot.
- L4 (13-16): Well-controlled structure with a clear build. Effects are chosen and mostly effective (e.g. withheld information, shifts in pace, contrast, a twist or reversal). Developed, specific detail. Ending is planned and connects to the opening or theme. Minor lapses only.
- L5 (17-20): Sophisticated control throughout. Structural and tonal choices are precise and sustained, and create a clear reader response (surprise, tension, humour, pathos). Ideas are developed with economy. Ending is deliberate and resonant. Very few lapses.

## AO5 descriptors (vocabulary and accuracy, 10 marks)
Ask: (a) Is vocabulary precise and varied, not just "big" or repeated? (b) Are sentences varied in length and structure, and used for effect? (c) Is paragraphing purposeful? (d) How many SPaG errors appear, and do they block meaning?

- L1 (1-2): Basic, repetitive vocabulary. Simple sentences throughout. Frequent errors that sometimes impede meaning.
- L2 (3-4): Some varied vocabulary. Some variation in sentence structure. Noticeable errors, but meaning usually clear.
- L3 (5-6): Mostly accurate. Vocabulary chosen with some care. Sentences varied, with occasional effective short sentences. Errors are occasional.
- L4 (7-8): Precise, varied vocabulary. Sentence variety is controlled and used for effect. Paragraphing is purposeful. Errors are rare and minor.
- L5 (9-10): Sophisticated, controlled vocabulary and syntax. Sentence choices create effect. Near-error-free, with any slips minor.

## Calibration rules
- Length, a dramatic premise, or lots of events do not earn marks. Plot events without control are L1-L2 for AO4.
- A clean, correct answer with flat, summary-style narration is at most L2 for AO4.
- A twist, reversal, or deliberate ending that ties the piece together is a strong marker of L4-L5 for AO4.
- If errors are frequent enough to impede meaning, AO5 cannot exceed L2.
- Score each answer independently. Do not compare answers while scoring.

## Output format (one line per answer)
ID | AO4 level | AO4 mark | AO5 level | AO5 mark | TOTAL | one-line justification with quoted evidence
