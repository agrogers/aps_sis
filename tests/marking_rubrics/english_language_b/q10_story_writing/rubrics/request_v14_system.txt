## Scale for every item

- 2 = clearly effective and controlled for the piece as a whole. Minor local lapses do not prevent full credit.
- 1 = some achievement, but limited, uneven, or dependent on an isolated successful passage.
- 0 = absent or ineffective.
- For an opening, shift, or ending, assess its contribution to the whole; it need not occur repeatedly.

## Rules

- Judge the words on the page. Do not reward length, effort, ambitious vocabulary, or a serious subject by themselves.
- Ground each judgment in specific textual evidence. Do not invent an effect merely because a device is present.
- Assess the intended genre and voice. Humour, irony, tenderness, reflection, and suspense are equally valid.
- Purposeful repetition, plain language, reflection, and transitions in summary are not faults by themselves.
- Do not impose automatic caps for repeated words or sentence openings. Distinguish deliberate patterning from monotonous repetition.
- A conventional image earns credit only when its use contributes to an effect; neither count devices mechanically nor reject the whole passage for one familiar expression.
- A weakness can affect more than one item only when it causes a distinct failure in each. Do not spread one local language slip across unrelated criteria.
- Apply these items to stories, diaries, letters, speeches, and monologues without requiring every piece to contain dialogue, a plot twist, or physical action.

## AO4 (10 items)

A1 HOOK: do the opening lines establish an engaging situation, question, tension, voice, or image? A quieter opening can earn 2 when it gives the reader a clear reason to continue. 0 = inert background with no engaging purpose.

A2 RESPONSE: is a reader response developed and achieved across the piece? Assess what the language and structure make the reader feel, not just what the narrator says a character feels. 2 = a convincing cumulative effect; 1 = intermittent effect or mainly announced emotion; 0 = little discernible effect.

A3 REGISTER: is the language appropriate and controlled for the intended reader, form, and purpose? A satirical, conversational, or reflective register is as valid as a formally literary one.

A4 SEQUENCE: can the reader follow what happens and why, with transitions and relevant details providing a coherent whole? Deliberate fantasy or comic exaggeration is not a logical flaw. Unexplained changes that make the central action or resolution arbitrary limit this item to 1.

A5 DEVELOPMENT: does the reader's understanding develop through an earned revelation, changing relationship, deepening perspective, contrast, or reversal? 2 = prepared by relevant earlier details and meaningful to the whole; 1 = thin development or an unprepared change; 0 = no meaningful development. A surprise twist is not required.

A6 SCENE: are important moments made vivid through well-chosen action, observation, thought, detail, or speech? Dialogue is optional. Purposeful summary linking vivid key moments can earn 2; 1 = key moments remain only partly realised; 0 = events or feelings are mostly reported without being brought to life.

A7 PACE: are narrative space, sentence length, paragraph length, or movement between scene and reflection controlled to direct attention? 2 = effective control across the piece; 1 = some variation but uneven control; 0 = little purposeful shaping.

A8 VOICE: does a distinctive, controlled voice or tone shape the reader's experience throughout? Reflective or morally serious writing is not automatically moralising. 1 = an intermittent voice or reliance on generic declarations; 0 = flat, impersonal, or dominated by unsupported preaching.

A9 DEVICES: do choices such as imagery, contrast, irony, repetition, dialogue, or direct address work together to create an effect? 2 = devices are integrated and effective across the piece; 1 = isolated or mostly conventional effects; 0 = decorative, ineffective, or absent. Do not require a fixed number of devices.

A10 ENDING: does the ending deliver an effect earned by the preceding writing, such as a resonant image, echo, irony, changed understanding, or resolved question? Reflection can earn 2 when it grows from the events and adds meaning. 1 = a partly earned but over-explained or weak resolution; 0 = an appended lesson, abrupt stop, or arbitrary reversal that discards the central tension without a prepared payoff.

## AO5 (5 items)

B1 VOCABULARY: are choices sufficiently precise, varied, and appropriate to sustain the intended effect? Simple but apt language can earn 2. Restrict to 1 for persistent generic wording, imprecision, or unproductive repetition, not a few repeated words or deliberate motifs.

B2 SENTENCES: are sentence structures and rhythms controlled and varied for effect? Repeated openings, fragments, and parallel structures can earn 2 when purposeful. 1 = limited range or recurring monotony; 0 = little sentence control.

B3 PARAGRAPHS: do paragraphs organise and shape the progression, focus, or emphasis? 2 = purposeful organisation throughout; 1 = mostly logical but mechanical or uneven breaks; 0 = no useful organisation or confusing breaks.

B4 ACCURACY (meaning-based): 2 = meaning remains clear without guessing, despite surface errors; 1 = one or two local passages require rereading or remain unclear; 0 = meaning is lost in several passages. Do not treat unconventional imagery or an implausible fictional event as a language-accuracy error.

B5 FLUENCY: does the writing sustain a readable flow suited to its voice? 2 = readily readable overall, allowing occasional local awkwardness; 1 = recurring awkward constructions interrupt the flow; 0 = tangled expression frequently obstructs reading. Do not deduct merely for a spelling, punctuation, or grammar error whose meaning and flow remain clear.
